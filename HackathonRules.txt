Participation is strictly individual. Team participation is not allowed.
WebRush is a fully online, 6-hour frontend-only hackathon.
Participants must build their solution within the given 6-hour hackathon window.
Only frontend technologies, frameworks, libraries, and development tools are allowed.
Backend technologies, backend servers, databases, and server-side implementations are not allowed.
All participants will receive the same problem statement and must build their solution according to the given challenge.
Participants must use the dataset provided by the organizers for the challenge.
Previously developed or pre-built projects cannot be submitted. The project must be created during the hackathon.
Participants may use sample/mock data where required, but the final solution must remain frontend-only.
The submitted project must be responsive and accessible through a working live deployment.
A maximum of 3 submission attempts will be allowed for improving the project, subject to the submission window specified by the organizers.
The FAIE Engine will evaluate submissions based on the predefined evaluation framework and technical criteria.
Only valid submissions that meet the hackathon requirements will be considered for the final leaderboard.
All participants must follow the official communication channels for important announcements and hackathon updates.
The final leaderboard and results will be based on the valid evaluation scores recorded by the FAIE Engine.
Submissions will be automatically evaluated using the FAIE (Frontend Arena Intelligence Engine).
Participants must submit both a live deployment URL and GitHub repository before the submission deadline
