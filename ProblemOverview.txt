Problem Statement
Your Life, In Receipts
The Concept

Your digital life is made up of hundreds of tiny moments.

A song you played at 2 AM.
A place you visited.
A photo you took.
Something you bought.
A movie you watched.
A message you saved.
A search you made.
A random note you wrote.

Individually, these moments may not mean much.

But when you put them together, they tell a story.

The Challenge

Build a frontend-only digital experience that transforms a collection of fictional “life receipts” into a meaningful and interactive story.

Every participant will receive the same dataset containing different types of digital-life activities:

Music
Movies & Entertainment
Places
Purchases
Photos
Messages
Searches
Events
Personal Notes

The dataset represents fragments of someone's digital life over a period of time.

Your task is not simply to display these records.

You must explore the data, identify meaningful connections, discover patterns, and turn those disconnected moments into a story that the user can understand and interact with.

The Core Challenge

Think about what could be hidden inside the data.

What kind of person was the user during different periods?

How did their interests and behaviour change over time?

Which moments are connected?

What recurring patterns can be found?

Can unrelated-looking receipts reveal a bigger story when viewed together?

Your experience should move beyond:

Raw Data → Information

and aim for:

Raw Data → Insights → Connections → Story

How you achieve this is entirely up to you.

Don't Just Build a Timeline

A simple chronological list of receipts is not enough.

We don't want:

January → February → March → April

with a collection of cards underneath.

Think about the relationships between different types of information.

For example:

Song → Location → Photo → Purchase → Event

might appear to be five unrelated records.

But together, they could represent one meaningful moment or chapter in someone's life.

Your interface should make these connections discoverable, interactive, and meaningful.

Minimum Requirements

Your solution must provide:

A way to explore the provided life receipts
Meaningful filtering, searching, or navigation
At least one meaningful mechanism for discovering relationships or patterns
An interactive storytelling experience
A clear visual representation of the user's digital journey
Responsive design across different screen sizes

These requirements define the minimum experience.

How you solve the problem is completely up to you.

Creative Direction

Your solution could take the form of:

Digital Scrapbook
Interactive Timeline
Life Map
Data Story
Personal Museum
Interactive Journal
Game-like Experience
Data Visualization
AI-inspired Interface
Or something completely new

These are only examples — you are not required to follow any of them.

We want to see your interpretation of the problem.

The Real Challenge

You have a dataset full of disconnected moments.

Your job is to give those moments meaning.

Don't just show the user:

“What happened?”

Help them discover:

“What does it all mean?”

The strongest solutions will make the user curious enough to explore, connect the dots, and discover something they didn't notice at first glance.

The Constraint

You have exactly 6 hours to:

Understand → Design → Build → Refine → Deploy

Your solution must be created within the hackathon duration.

This is a frontend-only challenge. You are free to use modern frontend frameworks, libraries, UI tools, and development tools of your choice.

Backend implementation is not required and is not permitted.

Your Mission

One Dataset.
Hundreds of Moments.
Infinite Stories.
Six Hours to Uncover One.

What story will you uncover?
